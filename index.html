THERMODYNAMIC MANIFOLD — TMF

Interactive, browser-based thermodynamic cycle simulators built for teaching — real property tables (IAPWS-IF97 steam, NASA 7-coefficient gas polynomials), live parameter sliders, and explicit physical-limit checks (Carnot bound, knock limit, T₃ ceiling) instead of black-box numbers. Every constant is tagged [PROVEN] / [NUMERICAL] / [CONJECTURE] in the source, so you can see what to trust and what to argue with.

Rankine cycle · v3.2

Steam Power Cycle

Boiler, turbine, condenser, pump — IAPWS-IF97 saturation & superheat tables, entropic-mean Carnot reference, turbine exhaust quality, entropy-generation breakdown along the cycle.

Otto & Diesel · v3.3

Internal Combustion + Turbo

Variable specific heats (NASA polynomials), moist-air knock limit, and a forced-induction module (compressor, charge-air cooling, condensation under boost) — includes a worked example on cooling vs. T₃/NOx trade-offs.

→ open simulator
Full chain · v2.2

Biomass Plant — Combustion to Grid

The whole chain in one pipeline: combustion → boiler → Rankine turbine → generator. Includes a marginal-gain view that ranks every lever by what it is worth at the terminals — and shows how much of a local improvement survives the chain. Same IAPWS-IF97 core as the Rankine simulator.

→ open simulator
